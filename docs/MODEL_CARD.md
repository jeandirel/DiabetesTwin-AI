# Model Card — DiabetesTwin-AI v0.1

## Intended use

Student research, software demonstration, interactive education, and prototyping of a glucose digital-twin workflow.

## Not intended for

- diagnosis or screening;
- emergency decisions;
- medication or insulin dose selection;
- replacing a CGM, physician, diabetes educator, or validated medical device;
- claims about clinical benefit.

## Digital-twin engine

The simulator produces a 24-hour glucose-like trajectory at five-minute resolution from:

- personalized baseline glucose;
- carbohydrate sensitivity;
- activity sensitivity;
- circadian amplitude;
- stress and sleep parameters;
- meal carbohydrate events;
- physical-activity events.

Effects are represented with simple transparent response kernels plus correlated noise. This is a physiology-inspired model, **not** a validated physiological simulator such as UVA/Padova.

## Prediction model

`HistGradientBoostingRegressor` predicts glucose 30 minutes ahead using current/lagged glucose, time-of-day features, recent carbohydrate/activity summaries, stress, and sleep.

Training data is synthetic and generated by the twin itself. Consequently, the displayed MAE/RMSE proves only that the software pipeline can learn the synthetic process. It does not establish clinical accuracy.

## Validation required before research with real participants

- patient-level temporal train/validation/test separation;
- external validation on a different cohort/site;
- uncertainty calibration;
- subgroup analysis (age, sex, diabetes phenotype, treatment mode, sensor type, etc.);
- robustness to missing/irregular CGM samples;
- clinically meaningful error analyses around hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia;
- privacy, security, consent, governance, and regulatory review.

## Data

The default repository contains no personal health data. A suitable public research dataset for future experiments is PhysioNet **CGMacros** (45 participants: healthy, prediabetes, and type 2 diabetes; CGM, macronutrients, activity, and additional measurements).
